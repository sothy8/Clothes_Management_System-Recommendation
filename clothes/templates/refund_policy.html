Refund & Return Policy

Easy returns, happy customers

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Return Window

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return items for a full refund. Items must be returned in their original condition with all tags attached.

2. Eligible Items

Most items can be returned, except:

  • Underwear and intimate apparel
  • Swimwear (unless defective)
  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Customized or personalized items
  • Items damaged by normal wear

3. Return Condition Requirements

To be eligible for a return, items must be:

  • Unused and in the same condition as received
  • In original packaging with all tags attached
  • Free from any odors (perfume, smoke, etc.)
  • Not washed or altered in any way

4. How to Return

1

Contact Us

Email us at returns@artisankh.com or call (123) xxxxxxxxxx to initiate your return.

2

Get Return Label

We'll provide you with a prepaid return shipping label and instructions.

3

Ship Your Return

Package your items securely and ship using the provided label.

4

Get Refunded

Once we receive and process your return, your refund will be issued within 5-7 business days.

5. Refund Processing

Refunds will be processed to your original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5-7 business days
  • PayPal: 3-5 business days
  • Store Credit: Immediately upon processing

Please note that it may take additional time for your bank to process the refund.

6. Exchanges

We offer exchanges for different sizes or colors of the same item, subject to availability. To exchange an item:

  • Follow the return process above
  • Specify you want an exchange in your return request
  • We'll ship the new item once we receive your return

7. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Provide photos of the damage/defect
  • We'll arrange immediate replacement or full refund
  • Return shipping is free for damaged/defective items

8. International Returns

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or damaged. Customs duties and taxes are non-refundable.

9. Sale Items

Sale items can be returned within 30 days for store credit only, unless otherwise specified. Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned.
